# Portfolio Content Brief

This file captures the information needed before calling the homepage "world class". The current page is a visual and structural foundation, not a final personal brand narrative.

## 1. Add / remove / keep

- What should be removed from the current homepage?
- What must stay because it is accurate and important?
- What feels too generic, exaggerated, or off-brand?
- Should the tone be serious, elite, minimal, playful, academic, startup-like, or engineering-focused?

## 2. Projects

For each project that should appear, provide:

- Project name
- One-sentence plain-English description
- Your role
- Current status: live, in progress, archived, private, open source
- Tech stack
- Links: demo, GitHub, article, screenshots, Telegram bot, app store, etc.
- Best measurable outcome: users, speed improvement, revenue, deployments, datasets, accuracy, uptime, contest result, or another proof point
- What makes the project technically impressive?

## 3. Achievements and milestones

Add concrete milestones such as:

- Awards, scholarships, admissions, certificates, or exam scores
- Shipped products or public launches
- Open-source contributions
- User/customer numbers
- Hackathons or competitions
- Research, datasets, ML experiments, or publications
- Work experience, freelance contracts, or internships

## 4. Personal positioning

Answer these before final copywriting:

- Who is the target visitor: recruiter, university reviewer, client, collaborator, investor, or general audience?
- What should they believe after 20 seconds?
- What should they do next: message on Telegram, email, view GitHub, read case studies, download CV, or something else?
- What location and identity details should be public?

## 5. Assets needed

A stronger final page may need:

- Real portrait or avatar
- Project screenshots
- Product logos or icons
- CV/resume PDF
- GitHub, LinkedIn, Telegram, email, and other profile links
- Updated domain/email confirmation

## 6. Final homepage sections to choose from

Possible sections:

- Hero with one precise positioning statement
- Featured project case studies
- Achievement timeline
- Technical stack
- Testimonials or references
- Writing/articles
- Contact block
- CV download
